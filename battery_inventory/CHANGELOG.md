# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First public release.

- Equipment, stock, dead batteries, history, settings (formats and categories).
- Liquid-glass interface, light and dark, phone and desktop.
- Undo (30 steps), kept across restarts.
- Replacement dates and reminders; lifetime estimates and yearly shopping estimate.
- Home Assistant entities through MQTT: sensors, switches, buttons; commands from Home Assistant.
- Backup and restore, daily automatic copy.
